Cerebrum

The cerebrum helps us think, learn, remember, understand language, and control voluntary movements.

Cerebellum

The cerebellum helps us keep our balance and coordinate our movements.

Brain Stem

The brain stem helps control important functions such as breathing and heart rate.

Diencephalon

The diencephalon includes the thalamus and hypothalamus. It helps process sensory information and regulate body functions.
